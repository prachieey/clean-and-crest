# Customer Review Request Templates

Use these templates to ask all customers for honest feedback following completed cleaning services.

### Ethical Review Collection Policy
- **No Incentives:** Never offer discounts, prizes, entry into competitions, or gifts in exchange for reviews. Australian consumer law (ACCC guidelines) prohibits incentivised reviews.
- **No Review Gating:** Send these requests to **all** customers regardless of whether their experience was positive or challenging. Never filter customers by pre-screening ratings.
- **Honest Feedback:** Encourage honest, candid feedback so customers can make informed choices and Clean & Crest can continuously maintain high service standards.

---

## 1. SMS Template

**Timing:** Send 2 to 4 hours after service completion.

```text
Hi [First Name], thank you for choosing Clean & Crest today! We hope your clean went smoothly.

Could you please take 30 seconds to leave us an honest Google review? Your feedback helps us maintain our high service standards:
[https://cleanandcrest.com.au/review]

Thank you,
Clean & Crest Team
```
